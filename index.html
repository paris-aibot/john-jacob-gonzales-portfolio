An integrated view // drawing board to live track.

I'm a Testing & Commissioning Engineer at the MRT-7 depot, where I verify that mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire-protection systems work the way they were designed to before the trains and passengers ever rely on them.

Concurrently, I serve as a certified Train Operator on the MRT-7 line and as an Associate Safety Officer. That mix is unusual — and intentional. It means I can walk a commissioning checklist with the same eyes that read a live cab signal, and spot operational risks long before they show up in service.

Lately I've been pushing into process automation and data tooling — Power Automate, Power Apps, Power BI — and building AI agents and small applications to take repetitive work off engineering teams. I'm interested in where infrastructure meets software.
